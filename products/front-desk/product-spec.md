# Front Desk — Complete Product Specification & Positioning Bible

> **The messaging half of your front desk, running on its own.**

---

## Executive Summary
**Front Desk** is an autonomous AI receptionist that answers every WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger enquiry a practice receives — in seconds, at any hour — and books the appointment straight into their calendar against live availability. It holds the conversation on its own. The staff can step in and take over at any point with a single tap.

---

## 1. The Core Problem It Solves

### The Leaking Bucket
* **Every unanswered message is an appointment booked somewhere else:** Patients do not wait. They message three practices and book with whichever one replies first. The winner is rarely the best clinician — it is whoever answered.
* **Busiest hours are the least responsive hours:** Enquiries arrive while the receptionist is checking a patient in, on the phone, or gone for the day. Saturday afternoon, Sunday evening, and after 6 PM are when people finally have time to sort out their treatment — and exactly when nobody is on the desk.
* **The gap is invisible:** A missed call leaves a missed call log. A message answered four hours late leaves nothing. The practice never finds out the patient booked elsewhere, so the revenue leak never shows up in reports or practice meetings.

### The Quantified Cost of Missed Enquiries (Illustrative Dental Economics)
Take one routine dental procedure at **€700 / ₹60,000 / $750**:
* **4 unanswered enquiries / month:** €2,800/month leak (**€33,600 / year**)
* **8 unanswered enquiries / month:** €5,600/month leak (**€67,200 / year**)
* **Fixing this requires zero ad spend:** The patients already found the practice. They already messaged. The only failure point was response time.

## 2. Features — Live Today (WhatsApp & Facebook Messenger)

### A. Autonomous Answering
* **Sub-second replies, 24/7/365:** 7:00 AM, 11:00 PM, Sunday, bank holidays. The enquiry is answered while the patient is still holding the phone.
* **Natural conversation (No rigid menus):** Not a decision tree, not an auto-reply, not a static web form. It asks what the patient needs, answers accurately, and steers toward booking.
* **Full practice knowledge:** Answers treatment costs, what a price includes, opening hours, what a first visit involves, which clinician handles what, directions, and parking.
* **Staffing & availability intelligence:** Knows who is in, who performs which procedure, and when they are next free.
* **Zero supervision required:** Runs without requiring staff to monitor, approve replies, or remain on standby.

### B. Filling the Diary (Autonomous Booking)
* **Live calendar integration:** Books directly into practice calendar slots against live availability. Patients leave with confirmed appointments, not promises of a callback.
* **Pre-appointment confirmation:** Automatic reminders reduce empty chairs caused by no-shows or forgotten times.
* **Lapsed lead reactivation:** Re-engages conversations that stalled at "how much is it?" instead of letting them fade.
* **Cancellation salvage:** When a patient says "I can't make Tuesday", Front Desk offers 3 alternative slots immediately instead of losing the booking.
* **Clinical audit log:** Logs every conversation, booking, and follow-up so the team starts the day with complete visibility.

### C. Promoting Active Offers Contextually
* Puts current practice promotions into the conversation naturally in context.
* Ensures 100% of enquirers hear about relevant offers, not just those who catch a receptionist on a slow afternoon.

### D. Human Control & Safety Guardrails
* **One-tap human override:** Any staff member can step into a live conversation mid-sentence. Front Desk pauses immediately and hands over the thread.
* **Strict calendar boundary:** Never commits beyond allowed parameters; availability strictly mirrors connected calendar rules.
* **Custom practice calibration:** Configured with exact clinic pricing, policies, tone, and FAQ before going live.

## 3. Operational Transformation: Before vs. After

| Operational Area | How It Works Today (Without Front Desk) | With Front Desk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekend & Evening Inquiries** | Message arrives 8 PM Saturday → answered Monday 10 AM. Patient already booked competitor. | Answered in 5 seconds; appointment confirmed before patient leaves WhatsApp. |
| **Simultaneous Channels** | Receptionist on the phone → WhatsApp/Messenger messages sit unread. | Chat never queues behind phone calls. 100 concurrent chats handled simultaneously. |
| **Repetitive FAQs** | "How much is a crown/implant?" typed out 20 times a day by desk staff. | Answered instantly and consistently with zero staff labor. |
| **Cold / Stalled Inquiries** | Inquiries that went quiet are permanently forgotten. | Automated, respectful re-engagement sequences recover stalled leads. |
| **Staffing & Coverage** | Rota management, sick leave, lunch cover, overtime costs. | Messaging desk operates 24/7 with zero sick days, leaves, or rota gaps. |
| **Out of Hours** | Closed. | Always open for bookings. |

## 4. Upcoming Roadmap — Voice (Not Live Today)

> **CRITICAL SALES & MARKETING RULE:** Front Desk today is **Chat-only**. Voice is on the build list and is **not yet released**. Never sell or market voice as a live feature.

### Planned Inbound Calling Features (Roadmap)
* Inbound call answering covering the same practice FAQ knowledge base.
* Direct CRM call notes transcription.
* Unified patient record across WhatsApp chat and phone call history.

*Positioning guidance:* If phone triage is the primary blocker for a prospect, state clearly: *"Front Desk solves the messaging half completely today; voice is currently in development."*

## 5. Target Markets & Ranking

1. **Tier 1 (Lead GTM): Multi-branch Dental & Medical Clinics, Real Estate**
   * Highest enquiry volume and high ticket value per booking.
   * Structural pain: Multiple locations, multiple numbers, multiple inboxes with no unified ownership.
2. **Tier 2: Diagnostic Labs, Physiotherapy, Dermatology, Cosmetic Clinics, Dental Chains**
   * High ratio of inquiries to bookings; response speed directly impacts top-line revenue.
3. **Tier 3: Gyms, Fitness Studios, Coaching & Tuition Centers**
   * Inquiries arrive predominantly out of hours (evenings/weekends when desks are unstaffed).
4. **Tier 4: Salons, Spas & Wellness Centers**
   * High message volume at moderate tickets; ROI case rests on staff time recovered and 24/7 capture.

---

## 6. What Front Desk is NOT (Anti-Slop Boundaries)

* ❌ **Not a phone system** (Chat-only today; Voice is future roadmap).
* ❌ **Not a button-and-menu chatbot** (No rigid decision trees or "Press 1 for pricing").
* ❌ **Not a replacement for clinical receptionists** (Takes the screen/messaging workload so staff can focus on in-clinic patient care).
* ❌ **Not a clinical diagnostic tool** (Answers practice logistics and pricing only; never diagnoses conditions or gives medical advice).

# Front Desk — The Missed Enquiry Leak & ROI Framework

---

## 1. The Core Economic Formula

$$\text{Monthly Revenue Leak} = (\text{Missed Out-of-Hours Enquiries}) \times (\text{Average Procedure Value}) \times (\text{Historical Conversion Rate})$$

---

## 2. Benchmark Procedure Values & Economics

### A. Dental Practices (UK / EU / US / India)
* **Routine Procedure Average:** €700 / $750 / ₹25,000–₹60,000 (Implant, Invisalign consultation, crown, root canal package).
* **4 missed weekend/evening enquiries / month:** 
  * **€2,800 / month** ($3,000 / mo | ₹2,40,000 / mo)
  * **€33,600 / year** ($36,000 / yr | ₹28,80,000 / yr)
* **8 missed weekend/evening enquiries / month:** 
  * **€5,600 / month** ($6,000 / mo | ₹4,80,000 / mo)
  * **€67,200 / year** ($72,000 / yr | ₹57,60,000 / yr)

---

### B. Medical, Aesthetic & Cosmetic Clinics
* **Average Treatment Ticket:** €1,200 / $1,500 / ₹50,000
* **5 missed leads / month:** €6,000/month = **€72,000 / year**

---

### C. Salons, Spas & Fitness Studios
* **High-volume / Lower ticket model:**
  * Average monthly membership / package: €120 / $150 / ₹5,000
  * 25 missed out-of-hours weekend queries: €3,000/month = **€36,000 / year**

---

## 3. The Front Desk Payback Math
* **Cost of Implementation:** ₹2L – ₹5L ($2,500 – $6,000 / €2,300 – €5,500)
* **Payback Period:** **1 to 2 recovered procedures** pays back the entire system within the first 30 days.
* **No ad spend required:** Recovers existing pipeline leakage from traffic that already landed on the practice's WhatsApp or Facebook channels.

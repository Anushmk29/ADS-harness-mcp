## 3. The Production Systems Portfolio (11 Shipped Systems)

### System 01: Front Desk (Flagship Product)
* **What it does:** The messaging half of your front desk running on its own across WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.
* **Capabilities:** 24/7 instant conversational replies, live calendar slot booking, stalled lead re-engagement, multi-branch routing, and 1-tap staff takeover.
* **Tech:** WhatsApp Business API, Facebook Messenger, Booking Calendar API, Multi-branch Routing.

### System 02: Competitive Intelligence Platform
* **What it does:** A market workspace showing every ad competitors run, which format is scaling, active ad counts, hook shifts, and unaddressed market whitespace.
* **Tech:** Ad library scrapers, web monitoring, workspace dashboards, alerting.

### System 03: Market Sentiment Intelligence
* **What it does:** Reads market discussions (e.g. Facebook community groups) overnight, volume-weights community mood, and delivers a bilingual executive decision report at 6:00 AM before business opens.
* **Tech:** Overnight ingestion, volume-weighted scoring, confidence labeling, Thai/English reporting.

### System 04: Devotional Archive Intelligence (Vishvas Foundation Case Study)
* **What it does:** Two decades of recorded Hindi discourse (40,000+ recordings, 3,015+ hours) turned into a searchable, citable corpus and voice synthesis layer — running 100% offline on a single on-premise workstation GPU.
* **Tech:** WhisperX large-v3, wav2vec2, Ollama, Qdrant, Tantivy, PostgreSQL, RTX 5090.
* **Metrics:** 9,402 recordings indexed, 24,629 cited passages, 19x faster than real-time, ₹0/min running cost, 100% place accuracy, consent gate fails closed.

### System 05: Outbound Lead Generation
* **What it does:** Discovers ICP decision-makers, enriches accounts via Apollo & Sales Navigator, scores intent, and drafts hyper-personalized copy for a human-approved send queue.
* **Tech:** n8n, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, GPT-4, CRM.

### System 06: AI Voice Follow-Up Agent
* **What it does:** Calls warm inbound form leads back within 2 minutes, qualifies interest, books calendar meetings, and logs full transcripts in the CRM.
* **Tech:** Vapi, n8n, Telephony APIs, Calendar, CRM.

### System 07: WhatsApp Qualification & Booking
* **What it does:** Filters out casual tire-kickers, scores buying intent, and confirms booked appointments on WhatsApp.
* **Tech:** WhatsApp Business API, n8n, Calendar, CRM.

### System 08: Customer Support & Knowledge Assistant
* **What it does:** Grounded single-layer knowledge retrieval across web chat, WhatsApp, and internal staff to eliminate repetitive FAQs.
* **Tech:** Vector knowledge base, WhatsApp API, Web Widget, CRM analytics.

### System 09: Competitor & Campaign Intelligence
* **What it does:** Monitors competing brands' active offers, creative pivots, and landing page copy changes with weekly digests and same-day alerts.
* **Tech:** Ad libraries, web monitoring, Slack/Email alert bots.

### System 10: Content Production & Publishing
* **What it does:** Multi-channel content repurposing pipeline turning one core research brief into platform-native drafts with named human sign-off.
* **Tech:** OpenClaw, Claude, GPT-4, Notion, WordPress, n8n.

### System 11: Ad Performance Reporting
* **What it does:** Ingests Meta & Google Ads performance daily to surface budget risks and fatigue before spend is wasted.
* **Tech:** Meta Ads API, Google Ads API, Google Sheets, Slack alerts.

Findings

What the numbers say

Worked

One Instagram-only video campaign made every sale

New Sales Campaign ran only on Instagram, with the "A little embroidery, a lot of elegance" video and a Shop Now button. Both orders came on 27 and 28 Oct. ₹1,045 per order is under the ₹1,900 you can afford per order, if the 50% margin in your business profile is right; that margin is still marked to confirm. Meta rated the video Above average for quality and engagement.

Two orders is too few to call it a winner. The harness creative check returned "wait for evidence" for the same reason.

Worked

WhatsApp chats cost ₹5–11 each, the cheapest thing you bought

The two WhatsApp campaigns spent ₹1,131 and started 137 chats, ₹8.26 on average. 47 of them came from WhatsApp Status placements, which cost only ₹146. About a third of the people sent a second message.

Meta can't see whether a chat became an order, so cost per WhatsApp order is unknown. Count orders that came through WhatsApp for each campaign and that number becomes your real cost per order.

Leak

56% of spend reached men

Every ad set is set to women, but Advantage+ audience was allowed to treat gender as a suggestion, so Meta widened it. Men got ₹4,742 of ₹8,475. Women started 114 of the 164 chats where gender is known; men started 50. Engagement 2.0 already has this switched off.

Both website orders were credited to men aged 18–24. That could be gifting or a shared phone. Two orders can't show a pattern either way.

Leak

Audience Network bought 59% of page views and no orders

Placements outside Facebook and Instagram (Audience Network) took ₹848 and gave 680 of the 1,154 landing page views, but no orders. The June carousel's 13.7% click rate is the usual sign of accidental taps in apps and games.

Three Phase (June) left all placements on, including Audience Network, Messenger and Threads.

Leak

₹2 reach that nobody watched

The awareness campaign bought 4.1 lakh impressions at ₹2.07 per thousand. Only 0.03% clicked, people watched for 1 second on average, and 7% stayed past 3 seconds. Uttar Pradesh took 3.8 lakh of the account's 5.8 lakh impressions, most of them from this campaign.

Leak

The October carousel cost ₹667 per thousand impressions

In Sales_11oct, Meta put ₹1,530 of ₹1,649 on the suit-set carousel at ₹667 per 1,000 impressions, about 10 times the account's usual cost. It got 42 page views and no orders. The videos in the same campaign got page views for ₹2–3 each. The campaign was paused after 4 days, before Meta could shift the budget.

Structure

Nine campaigns, none given a full week

The longest any campaign spent was 10 days, and most ran 2–6. The harness playbook says to judge only after at least 7 days. Meta's learning phase needs about 50 results a week. At ₹4,237 per order that's impossible on this budget, but WhatsApp chats can reach it: 50 chats at ₹8 is about ₹400 a week.

Structure

Optimised for the wrong events

Three Phase optimised for add to cart and view content, so Meta found people who add to cart and leave: 19 carts, 0 orders. The August catalog campaign used a cost cap that was set too low, so it never won auctions and spent ₹6 in 10 days.

Audience

Younger than the buyer you want

18–24-year-olds got 36% of spend (₹3,084). Your profile names 27–38 as the core buyer. Most ad sets allowed ages 18–65.

Fine

No creative fatigue

Frequency stayed between 1.0 and 1.4 everywhere. No ad ran long enough for people to tire of it, so the problem is reaching the right people, not worn-out creative.

Website funnel

Where visitors dropped off

Next 30 days

A plan that fits ₹5,000 a month

  1. Week 1: Fix billing. Decide on New Sales Campaign (fix 1). Turn off gender widening and Audience Network (fixes 4–5). Lower Engagement 2.0 to about ₹100 a day so the month's budget lasts, then switch it on.
  2. Week 2: Leave it alone for 7 full days. Log WhatsApp orders. Read cost per chat and how many chats reach a second message.
  3. Week 3: Keep the ad that gets the cheapest chats. Add one new video with an opening built like the engagement video.
  4. Week 4: Work out cost per WhatsApp order. If it's under ₹1,900, raise the budget 20–25%. If not, change the offer or the copy, not the audience.

Watch every week

  • Cost per WhatsApp chat: target under ₹12
  • Share of chats that reach a 2nd message: about a third now, aim for 40%
  • WhatsApp orders per week, and spend divided by those orders
  • Share of spend on women: target over 90%
  • 3-second hold rate on new videos: target over 25%
  • Cost per website order: target under ₹1,200 (the target in your business profile)

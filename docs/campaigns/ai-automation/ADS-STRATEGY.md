## 2. Why Meta Ads for B2B Services in India

| Factor | Meta Advantage in India |
|--------|------------------------|
| CPM | ₹80–250 (vs. LinkedIn ₹800–1,500) — 5–8x cheaper reach |
| Audience Size | 300M+ monthly active users in India |
| SMB Reach | India's SMB owners are heavily on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook |
| Retargeting | Website visitors, video viewers, lead-form engagers — all available |
| Lead Gen | Instant Forms reduce friction; WhatsApp pre-fill works well in India |
| Creative | Video demos, carousel case studies, UGC testimonials all work |
| Cost to Start | ₹200/day is viable; LinkedIn minimum viable is ₹50,000+/month |
| Language | Hindi + English creative; Meta supports both natively |

### Strategic Framing
**Phase A (Weeks 1–4):** Build awareness + retargeting audience at ₹200/day
**Phase B (Weeks 5+):** Add lead generation once audience is ready (₹350–500/day)

> **Why two phases?** At ₹200/day, splitting across 3 campaigns means each gets ~₹67/day — too little for Meta's algorithm to optimize. Building an audience first, then adding lead gen, is the most efficient use of a tight budget.

## 3. The Two-Phase Approach

### Phase A: Awareness + Audience Building (Weeks 1–4)
**Budget:** ₹200/day = ₹6,000/month
**Campaign:** 1 Awareness/Link Clicks campaign
**Goal:** Build a retargeting pool of 2,000–5,000 people who've seen your ads, visited your site, or watched your video
**What you get:** Cheap reach, video views, website visitors, brand recognition
**You do NOT get:** Many leads yet (that's Phase B)

### Phase B: Lead Generation (Weeks 5+)
**Budget:** ₹350–500/day = ₹10,500–15,000/month
**Campaigns:** 1 Lead Gen + 1 Retargeting (reusing Phase A audience)
**Goal:** Convert the warm audience into leads via Instant Forms
**What you get:** 30–100 leads/month at ₹150–500 CPL
**Why it works:** You're paying for leads from an audience that already knows you — conversion rates are 2–3x higher than cold prospecting

### Why Not Run Lead Gen From Day 1 at ₹200?
- ₹200 split across Lead Gen + Awareness = ₹100 each
- ₹100/day on Lead Gen = ~3–8 leads/day = ₹25–75/lead (actually not bad)
- BUT: Learning phase takes 50 conversions = 25–500 days at 1/day
- AND: No retargeting audience to feed back
- **Two-phase is faster to profitability** because Phase B leads come from a warm pool

## 4. Andromeda + GEM + Lattice: What This Means for Your Account

Meta's 2025–2026 delivery stack (Andromeda retrieval engine, GEM generative embeddings, Lattice sequence optimizer) has one critical implication:

> **Creative IS targeting.** The algorithm reads your ad content to find the right audience. You do NOT need (and should not over-rely on) narrow interest stacks.

**Practical rules for a fresh low-budget India account:**
1. **Broad targeting + strong creative** beats narrow targeting + mediocre creative
2. **Diversity > volume**: 3 genuinely different creative concepts > 30 minor variations
3. **Advantage+ Audience ON by default** — let Meta's retrieval engine do the matching
4. **Cross-format** (video + image + carousel) to maximize retrieval tickets
5. **Hindi + English creative** — test both; Hindi often performs better for SMB audiences in Tier 2/3 cities
6. **Avoid Similarity Score >60%** between creatives — cluster-mates share retrieval tickets

## 8. Conversion Strategy (Low-Budget Optimized)

### Primary Conversion: Lead Form Submission (Instant Form)
**Why Instant Forms first:**
- Zero landing page build required (saves time + money)
- 2–3x higher conversion rate than website forms for cold traffic
- Pre-filled fields (name, email, phone, **WhatsApp**) reduce friction
- **India advantage:** WhatsApp pre-fill works well; many SMB owners prefer WhatsApp over email
- Meta handles data delivery directly to you (email/CSV/webhook)

**Form Fields (keep it minimal):**
1. Full Name (pre-filled)
2. Work Email (pre-filled)
3. Phone / WhatsApp Number (pre-filled) ← **Critical for India**
4. City (dropdown: your service cities)
5. "What's the #1 process in your business you wish was automated?" (open text)

> **The open-text question is critical.** It filters tire-kickers and gives your sales team immediate context for the discovery call.

### Lead Qualification Flow (India-Optimized)
```
Lead Form Submit
    → Instant email + SMS notification (Meta → your phone)
    → Auto WhatsApp message: "Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in 
       AI automation. Can you book a 20-min call? [Calendly/WhatsApp link]"
    → Sales calls/WhatsApps within 2 hours (speed-to-lead matters in India)
    → Tag in CRM: Source=Meta, Campaign=, Creative=, LeadType=
    → Follow-up WhatsApp sequence if no call in 24h:
      Day 1: "Hi, just checking in. Any questions?"
      Day 3: "Here's a case study relevant to your industry"
      Day 7: "Last check — we have 2 slots left this week"
```

## 9. Tracking Stack (Minimal but Correct)

For a low-budget India launch, you need:

| Component | Priority | Effort |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Meta Pixel (base + standard events) | P1 | 1–2 hours |
| Meta CAPI (Conversions API) | P1 | 2–4 hours |
| Domain verification | P1 | 30 min |
| AEM (Aggregated Event Measurement) | P1 | 1 hour |
| Custom conversion: "Lead Form Submit" | P1 | 30 min |
| Custom conversion: "WhatsApp Click" | P1 | 30 min |
| CAPI Gateway (server-side) | P2 | 2–4 hours |
| Enhanced Matching (phone, email) | P1 | 1 hour |

> **Without CAPI, expect 30–40% conversion data loss from iOS ATT + ad blockers.** At ₹200/day, that's ₹60–80/day of invisible spend. CAPI is not optional.

**India-specific note:** Indian users are heavily on Android (75%+). iOS ATT impact is lower than US, but still present. CAPI is still recommended.

## 12. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Lead quality too low (tire-kickers) | High | High | Open-text form question; speed-to-lead <2hr via WhatsApp; qualify on first call |
| CPL too high for B2B | Medium | High | Shift budget to retargeting; narrow geo; test different angles |
| Creative fatigue within 3 weeks | Medium | Medium | Have 2 backup concepts ready; 21-day refresh cycle |
| No conversion data in first 2 weeks | High | Medium | Normal — learning phase; keep running; use Instant Forms for faster conversions |
| iOS/Android data loss (no CAPI) | Medium | High | CAPI is P1 — implement before launch |
| Budget too low for algorithm | High | Medium | ₹200/day is above minimum; consolidate to 1 ad set; be patient with learning phase |
| Indian SMB audience prefers WhatsApp over forms | Medium | Medium | Add WhatsApp CTA step; test WhatsApp click as secondary conversion |
| Language barrier (Hindi vs English) | Medium | Medium | Test both; Hindi often wins for Tier 2/3 SMB audiences |
| Special Ad Category restrictions | Low | Medium | AI services should NOT trigger SAC; verify during setup |

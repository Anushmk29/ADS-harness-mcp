# Marketing Psychology and Mental Models
Identify which models apply, explain the psychology, give the marketing application, suggest ethical implementation.

**Thinking models**: First Principles (5 Whys), Jobs to Be Done (a drill buyer wants a hole), Circle of Competence, Inversion (list what guarantees failure), Occam's Razor (check the broken form before attribution theories), Pareto 80/20, Local vs Global Optima, Theory of Constraints (fix the bottleneck: if traffic is low, conversion work will not help), Opportunity Cost, Diminishing Returns, Second-Order Thinking (flash sale trains discount waiting), Map is not Territory, Probabilistic Thinking, Barbell Strategy (80% proven, 20% experiments).

**Buyer psychology**: Fundamental Attribution Error, Mere Exposure, Availability Heuristic, Confirmation Bias, Lindy Effect, Mimetic Desire (waitlists, exclusivity), Sunk Cost, Endowment Effect (free trials), IKEA Effect, Zero-Price Effect, Hyperbolic Discounting (stress immediate benefit), Status-Quo Bias ("Import your data in one click"), Default Effect, Paradox of Choice (3 tiers beat 7), Goal-Gradient (progress bars), Peak-End Rule, Zeigarnik (open loops), Pratfall Effect ("We're not the cheapest, but..."), Curse of Knowledge, Mental Accounting ($3/day vs $90/month), Regret Aversion (guarantees), Social Proof.

**Persuasion**: Reciprocity, Commitment and Consistency, Authority, Liking/Similarity, Unity, Scarcity (only if genuine), Foot-in-the-Door, Door-in-the-Face, Loss Aversion (losses feel ~2x), Anchoring, Decoy Effect, Framing (90% success vs 10% failure), Contrast.

**Pricing**: Charm pricing ($99 vs $100), round prices signal premium, Rule of 100 (under $100 use % off, over $100 use $ off), Good-Better-Best, mental-accounting frames.

**Design and delivery**: Hick's Law (one CTA beats three), AIDA, Rule of 7 touchpoints, Nudge/choice architecture, BJ Fogg (Behaviour = Motivation x Ability x Prompt), EAST (Easy, Attractive, Social, Timely), COM-B, Activation Energy, North Star Metric, Cobra Effect.

**Growth**: Feedback loops, Compounding, Network effects, Flywheel, Switching costs, Exploration vs Exploitation, Critical mass, Survivorship bias.

Quick reference: low conversions (Hick, Activation Energy, Fogg), price objections (Anchoring, Framing, Mental Accounting, Loss Aversion), trust (Authority, Social Proof, Reciprocity, Pratfall), urgency (Scarcity, Loss Aversion, Zeigarnik), churn (Endowment, Switching Costs, Status-Quo), stalled growth (Constraints, Local vs Global, Compounding), paralysis (Paradox of Choice, Default, Nudge), onboarding (Goal-Gradient, IKEA, Commitment).

# Content Strategy
Every piece must be searchable, shareable, or both; search first. Treat each piece like a product launch (brand surface area).

**Searchable types**: use-case content ([persona] + [use case]), hub and spoke, template libraries. **Shareable types**: thought leadership, data-driven content, expert roundups (15-30 experts), case studies (Challenge, Solution, Results, Learnings), meta/behind the scenes. **Link-earning formats** (Foundation Inc., directional): statistics roundups 4.25x their page share of backlinks, glossary 1.47x, interactive tools 1.38x, how-tos 1.36x, original research 0.80x, ultimate guides 0.77x, thought leadership 0.74x, templates 0.68x. Curated stat pages beat original research for links; pair research with your own citable stat page.

**Pillars**: 3-5 topics you will own (product-led, audience-led, search-led, competitor-led), each with a cluster.
**Keyword modifiers by stage**: awareness (what is, how to, guide), consideration (best, vs, alternatives), decision (pricing, reviews, demo), implementation (templates, tutorial, setup).
**Ideation sources**: keyword exports, call transcripts, surveys, forums (Reddit, Quora, HN), competitor content, sales and support input.
**Prioritise** by Customer Impact 40%, Content-Market Fit 30%, Search Potential 20%, Resources 10% (score 1-10, weighted sum).
**Calendar split 60/30/10**: 60% searchable, 30% shareable, 10% experimental.
**Per format**: blog: write 10 titles first, ~5 edit passes; guide: the flagship, TOC, build hub before spokes; video: script hook first, cut clips at creation; podcast: designed to be atomised; email: one idea per send.
**Create once, distribute twice**: build distribution hooks in at creation (subheads that stand alone, liftable sections, pre-chosen pull quotes). ORB funnel: Borrowed (other audiences) for discovery, Rented (social) for engagement, Owned (email, blog, community) for conversion. Failure modes: spray-and-pray, platform dependency (Facebook organic reach fell from ~20% to under 2%), ownership paradox (90% of effort on channels you do not control).
**Output**: pillars with rationale, priority topics (title, searchable/shareable, type, keyword, stage, evidence), cluster map.

# A/B Testing
Start with a hypothesis: "Because [observation], we believe [change] will cause [outcome] for [audience]. We'll know when [metrics]." Test one thing. Pre-set sample size; do not peek. Metrics: primary tied to hypothesis, secondary for context, guardrail to stop harm. Sample per variant (baseline 3%): 10% lift ~47k, 20% ~12k, 50% ~2k; baseline 10%: 12k / 3k / 550. Allocation 50/50 default, 90/10 conservative. 95% confidence means p < 0.05. Checklist: hypothesis, primary metric, sample size, correct variants, tracking verified, QA. Analyse: sample reached, significant, effect meaningful, secondary consistent, guardrails, segment differences. Program: hypotheses from analytics, research, competitors, tickets, recordings, past tests; ICE prioritisation ((Impact + Confidence + Ease)/3); 4-8 experiments a month, 20-30% win rate, 20+ backlog; document each test as a playbook entry (hypothesis, result with CI, pattern, apply-to); weekly check for issues, bi-weekly conclude and launch, monthly velocity review, quarterly playbook audit.

# Lead Magnets
Solve one specific problem; match the buyer stage (awareness: checklist, cheat sheet, guide, quiz; consideration: comparison template, assessment, case studies, webinar; decision: templates, free trial, migration checklist, ROI calculator); high perceived value, consumable in under 30 minutes (ideally 10); natural path to product; one format. Types by effort: checklist (1-2h), cheat sheet, template (2-8h), swipe file, ebook (1-3 weeks), email mini-course, video course, quiz, webinar, resource library. Gating: full gate (max capture), partial, ungated, content upgrade (2-5x better than generic sidebar CTAs). Ask minimum fields; each extra field costs 5-10%. Landing page: benefit headline, preview mockup, 3-5 inside points, social proof, minimal form, FAQ. Thank-you page: confirm, next step, share, related content. Benchmarks: 20-40% conversion warm, 5-15% cold. Test headline, format, gate level, fields, CTA copy, delivery.

# Offer Design
The offer is the thing, not the page; better copy on a weak offer compounds slowly. **Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice).** Levers: raise outcome (specify and name it), raise likelihood (proof, guarantees, methodology), cut time delay (faster first win), cut effort (done-for-you, fewer decisions). **Six components**: core deliverable, bonus stack, guarantee, scarcity/urgency, name, price and payment structure. Diagnostic loop: identify business type, state the offer plainly, score the four levers 1-10 (lowest binds), audit the six components, fix one lever per iteration, project lift honestly (10-40% per component; 5x claims are selling something). Do not: fake scarcity, over-promise guarantees, inflate bonus value, use course-bro aesthetics, discount to acquire (discount-askers churn ~2x). Banned vocabulary: game-changing, revolutionary, disruptive, next-level, 10x, secret, hidden, "limited time" without a time limit, "worth $X" with no comparable, "100% guaranteed" without conditions. Use specific numbers, named customers, concrete outcomes.

# Launch Strategy
Launch again and again. **ORB framework**: Owned (email, blog, podcast, community; start with 1-2), Rented (social, app stores, YouTube, Reddit; pick 1-2 and funnel to owned), Borrowed (guest content, collaborations, speaking, influencers). **Readiness gate**: SLC, Simple (one thing), Lovable (people choose it), Complete (whole experience at its scope). Avoid Stealth Mode (launching too late) and "Just One More Feature". **Five phases**: internal, alpha (landing page and waitlist), beta (teasers, influencers), early access (leak screenshots, batches of 5-10%), full launch (self-serve, charging, all channels). Product Hunt: prepare relationships and listing, treat launch as an all-day event, reply to every comment, capture email; case: SavvyCal #2 Product of the Month, Reform #1 Product of the Day. Post-launch: onboarding emails, roundup, comparison pages, interactive demo. Update sizing: major (full campaign), medium (targeted email and banner), minor (changelog). Checklists: pre-launch, launch day, post-launch.

# CRO
Framework in order of impact: value proposition clarity (understand in 5 seconds), headline effectiveness, CTA placement/copy/hierarchy (weak "Submit", strong "Start Free Trial"), visual hierarchy and scannability, trust signals (logos, specific testimonials, case-study numbers) near CTAs, objection handling (price, "will this work for me", implementation, "what if it fails"), friction (form fields, unclear next steps, mobile, load time). Output: quick wins, high-impact changes, test ideas, 2-3 copy alternatives. Page types: homepage (positioning for cold visitors), landing (message match, single CTA), pricing (recommend a plan), feature (feature to benefit), blog (contextual CTAs).

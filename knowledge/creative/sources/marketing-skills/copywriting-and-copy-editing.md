# Copywriting
(Condensed from marketing-skills. references/copy-frameworks, natural-transitions, checklist, plain-english-alternatives, content-refresh not included.)

You are an expert conversion copywriter. Read the product marketing context first if present.

## Before writing
Page purpose (type, ONE primary action); audience (who, problem, objections, their language); product/offer (differentiation, transformation, proof); context (traffic source, what visitors already know).

## Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness.** Clear positioning is associated with +81% conversions, 38% shorter sales cycle, 28% lower CAC, 175% more referrals. Tools: the "Now you can" test, Human Action Model (discomfort, vision, path), the Perception Gap.
- Benefits over features. Specificity over vagueness ("Cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes"). Customer language over company language (mine reviews, interviews, tickets). One idea per section.

## Style rules
Simple over complex (use, help). Specific over vague (avoid streamline, optimize, innovative). Active over passive. Confident over qualified (drop almost, very, really). Show over tell. Honest over sensational (no fabricated stats or testimonials). Be direct; rhetorical questions engage ("Tired of chasing approvals?"); analogies make abstract concrete; humour only if it fits and never undermines clarity. Remove exclamation points and buzzwords.

## Page structure
Above the fold: **headline** (single most important message; formulas: "{Outcome} without {pain}", "The {category} for {audience}", "Never {unpleasant event} again", a question naming the pain), **subheadline** (expands, adds specificity, 1-2 sentences), **primary CTA** (action-oriented; communicates what they get). Core sections: social proof, problem/pain, solution/benefits (3-5), how it works (3-4 steps), objection handling (FAQ, guarantees), final CTA with risk reversal.

## CTAs
Weak: Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here, Get Started. Strong: Start Free Trial, Get [Specific Thing], See [Product] in Action, Create Your First [Thing], Download the Guide. Formula: action verb + what they get + qualifier.

## Page-specific
Homepage: serve multiple audiences without being generic, broadest value prop, paths per intent. Landing: single message and CTA, headline matches the ad, complete argument. Pricing: help choose, make the recommended plan obvious. Feature: feature to benefit to outcome. About: why you exist, still include a CTA.

## Voice
Set formality (casual / professional-friendly / formal), personality (playful/serious, bold/understated, technical/accessible). Headlines bolder, body clearer, CTAs action-oriented.

## Output
Page copy by section; annotations (why, which principle); 2-3 alternatives for headlines and CTAs; meta title and description if relevant.

# Copy Editing: the Seven Sweeps
Do not change the core message. Multiple focused passes beat one unfocused review; each edit needs a reason. After each sweep, loop back to check earlier sweeps.

1. **Clarity**: confusing structures, unclear pronouns, jargon, ambiguity, missing context. Confirm one main idea per section and that copy speaks to the reader ("You rule").
2. **Voice and tone**: shifts between formal and casual, mixed "we" and "the company", accidental humour shifts. Read aloud.
3. **So what**: every claim must answer "why should I care?". "AI-powered analytics" becomes "...surface insights you'd miss manually, so you can decide in half the time". Add "which means..." bridges.
4. **Prove it**: back every claim with evidence: named testimonials, case studies, statistics, third-party validation, guarantees, logos, review scores. "Trusted by thousands" (which?), "industry-leading" (according to whom?).
5. **Specificity**: replace vague with concrete (Save time -> Save 4 hours every week; Many customers -> 2,847 teams; Fast results -> Results in 14 days). Remove content that cannot be made specific; it is filler.
6. **Heightened emotion**: paint the before state, sensory language, micro-stories, shared experiences, reflective questions. Serve the message; never manipulate.
7. **Zero risk**: friction near CTAs, unanswered objections, missing trust signals, unclear next steps, hidden costs. Add money-back guarantee, free trial, "no credit card", "cancel anytime", social proof near CTA, clear expectations of what happens next.

## Expert panel scoring (high-stakes copy)
Assemble 3-5 personas (landing page: conversion copywriter, UX writer, target customer, brand strategist; email: email specialist, copywriter, spam-filter analyst, target customer; sales page: direct-response copywriter, skeptical buyer, editor, SEO specialist). Each scores 1-10 with specific critiques; revise lowest first; re-score until all 7+ and average 8+. Scale: 9-10 publish-ready, 7-8 minor tweaks, 5-6 another pass, 3-4 major revision, 1-2 rethink. Use for launch copy, pricing pages, high-traffic pages; recommended for sequences, sales pages, ads.

## Quick-pass checks
Cut: very, really, extremely, incredibly, just, actually, basically, "in order to", vague things/stuff. Replace: utilize->use, implement->set up, leverage->use, facilitate->help, innovative->new, robust->strong, seamless->smooth, cutting-edge->new. Watch adverbs, passive voice, nominalizations ("make a decision" -> "decide"). One idea per sentence, vary length, front-load, max ~25 words. One topic per paragraph, 2-4 sentences on web.

## Common problems and fixes
Wall of features (add "which means..."), corporate speak ("how would a human say this?"), weak opening (lead with the reader's problem or desired outcome), buried CTA, no proof, generic claims (who, how, by how much), mixed audiences (pick one), feature overload (3-5 benefits).

## Working with sweeps
Run a sweep and present findings with reasons; recommend specific edits; ask for updated copy; verify previous sweeps; repeat until a full sweep finds nothing.

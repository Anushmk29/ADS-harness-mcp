# Creative & Copy Frameworks (SaaS / B2B)

Goal: produce genuinely distinct angles (not word-swaps). Meta's Andromeda suppresses
near-duplicate creative, so diversity of *concept and motivator* matters more than volume.

## Step 1 - pick angles before writing a word
Generate 5-8 distinct angles from these motivator buckets, grounded in the user's ICP
pains/objections from config/business.yaml and the our-business-fit topic:
- **Pain/cost-of-inaction** ("Still doing X in spreadsheets?")
- **Outcome/ROI** ("Cut reporting time 80%")
- **Speed/ease** ("Live in 10 minutes, no engineer needed")
- **Authority/proof** (logos, stats, "rated 4.8 by 2,000 teams")
- **Comparison/switch** ("The [Competitor] alternative teams actually keep")
- **Objection-killer** (security, integrations, no lock-in, free migration)
- **Founder/insider POV** (thought-leadership, contrarian take)
- **Use-case specific** (one workflow for one role/vertical)

## Step 2 - funnel-stage matching
| Stage | Format | Message |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Awareness (cold) | short video, carousel | name the problem / category; teach, don't sell |
| Consideration | demo video, comparison | why you vs alternatives, specific features |
| Decision (retarget) | testimonial, case study | concrete results + proof; trial/demo CTA |

## Step 3 - write the copy (give multiple variants)

### Meta ad (per angle)
- **Primary text**: hook in first line (visible before "see more"), 1-2 supporting
  lines, 1 CTA. Lead with the pain or the outcome, not the product name.
- **Headline** (<=40 char): the promise. 3-5 variants.
- **Description** (optional): proof or risk-reducer.
- **CTA button**: "Sign Up" / "Get Quote" / "Learn More" matched to stage.

### Google RSA (per ad group)
- **12-15 headlines** (<=30 char): include primary keyword variants, 2-3 benefit
  headlines, 1-2 proof, 1-2 CTA, 1 brand. Don't over-pin (pin 1-2 max).
- **4 descriptions** (<=90 char): benefit, proof/stat, differentiator, CTA.
- Mirror the search intent: pricing-intent keyword -> mention pricing/trial in copy.

### Creative brief (for designers/video)
For each winning angle output: hook (first 3s), visual concept, on-screen text,
format + aspect ratios (9:16 + 1:1), proof element, and CTA. Note that SaaS performers
include: product demos (30-60s screen recordings), customer testimonial videos with
specific metrics, side-by-side comparison content, and founder/insider POV.

## Step 4 - testing discipline
- Test ONE variable at a time (hook, angle, format) - see the ppc-math topic for sample size.
- Refresh creative every 14-21 days on Meta (Andromeda compressed creative lifespan).
- Kill an ad only after enough data (>= ~50 clicks or 1-2x target CPA in spend).

## Compliance / brand
Respect the "avoid" list and proof points in config/business.yaml and the our-business-fit topic. No unverifiable
claims. If the user is in a restricted category (finance/credit), flag Special Ad
Category targeting limits before writing.

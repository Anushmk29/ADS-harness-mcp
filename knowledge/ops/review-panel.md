# Review panel: test an offer, landing page or campaign brief against four buyers

Use this before launching an offer, a landing page, a lead form or a campaign brief, and whenever the team is split on one.
It is a thinking tool, not evidence: a panel score never goes into `skill_learnings` as a result.

## Refuse to run without the basics
The piece must state all five:
1. Who it's for.
2. The price, or the price range.
3. What they get.
4. The channel.
5. How soon they see value.

If any is missing, list what's missing and stop.

## The four reviewers
Each reviewer reads the piece as that person, asks their three questions, gives a verdict and a score from 0 to 3, and names their one blocker.

| Reviewer | Their three questions | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner-buyer** (clinic owner or SME founder) | Does this fix a problem I have this month? Can I picture my team using it? What happens if it breaks? | "I'd book a call now" | "Interested, but X worries me" | "Right idea, wrong pitch" | "Not for someone like me" |
| **Finance approver** (partner, CFO, spouse who signs) | What does it cost in total, including retainer? When does it pay back, in ₹? What's the risk if we stop? | "Clear yes" | "Yes if the payback holds" | "Numbers unclear" | "Can't justify it" |
| **Skeptic** (burned by a past tech vendor) | What's the catch? What did they not say? Why them and not a cheaper tool? | "Low risk" | "Concerns they can answer" | "They avoided the real question" | "This will fail" |
| **Delivery lead** (our side) | Can we deliver this in the promised time? What would we have to descope? Does it need a hire? | "Deliverable now" | "Deliverable with descoping" | "Needs a hire" | "Wrong for our stack" |

## Verdict
The total is out of 12:
- **10–12:** ship.
- **7–9:** revise the named blockers.
- **4–6:** rework the offer or the angle.
- **0–3:** stop.

## Merging the reviews
- If 3 or 4 reviewers flag the same fix, apply it.
- If they disagree, list the disagreement as **the operator's call**; don't average it away.
- Always surface the skeptic's "question they avoided". It's usually the one prospects ask on the sales call.
- End with the three next actions, in order.
